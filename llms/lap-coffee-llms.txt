# LAP Coffee

> LAP Coffee ("Life Among People") is a Berlin-founded specialty coffee retail chain operated by Micro Retail Technologies MRT GmbH. It opened its first store in Berlin Mitte in August 2023 and now runs a network of small, high-throughput neighbourhood cafes across Berlin, with locations and openings in Cologne, Frankfurt, Munich and Hamburg. LAP is backed by HV Capital.

LAP Coffee does not publish a public API, developer portal, SDK or developer documentation. Its
technology surface is consumer-facing: the LAP: Everyday Coffee mobile app (pre-order, in-store
pickup, stamps and rewards, gifting, referrals), the `order.lap.coffee` deep-link/attribution
service that routes store QR codes into the app, and a Webflow marketing site. This file is
generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog profile, not fetched from
the provider — LAP publishes no `/llms.txt`.
